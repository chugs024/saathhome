Privacy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Saath doesn't collect your personal data. There are no Saath accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of ours. Everything you add lives in your own iCloud, shared only with the people you invite.

Who we are

Saath is made by Chugs ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the Saath apps for iPhone and Apple TV and for this website. Questions about privacy go to saath.help@gmail.com.

The Saath app

Your home's data

Everything you add to Saath (your home's name, your names and emoji, groceries, to-dos, chores, bills, saved stores, notes, the wall screen layout and the calendar plans you choose to share) is stored in your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. It is shared only with the people you invite through an iCloud share link. We can't see it, and it never passes through any server of ours.

You can stop sharing, or delete your home and everything in it, at any time in Saath's Settings.

Permissions you can choose to give

  • Calendar. Used to show the calendars you pick and to add plans you create. Plans from the calendars you pick (the next 7 days) are shared with your home, so the other phone and your Apple TV can show them.
  • Location ("While Using"). Used only so iOS can remind you of the grocery list when you arrive at a store you saved. Your location is never stored or sent anywhere by Saath.
  • Notifications. Used for updates from your home and reminders you turn on. They're created on your device.

Purchases

The free week and the one-time unlock are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Saath only asks the App Store whether your Apple Account has started the free week or bought Saath. We never see your payment details.

What stays on your device

A few preferences, like your appearance setting and which notifications you want, are kept on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking. The site stores nothing in your browser and runs no third-party scripts.
  • Hosting. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log your IP address and browser details to run and secure the service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We don't have access to visitor logs.

When you email us

If you write to saath.help@gmail.com, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use them only to reply to you, and we delete them on request. That mailbox is provided by Google, so Google processes it in transit and in storage.

What we don't do

We don't sell, rent or share personal information, and we don't use it for advertising or profiling. Saath isn't directed at children and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct or delete it, and to object to its use. Since your home's data is in your own iCloud, you control it directly in Saath. For anything else, write to saath.help@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If Saath ever starts collecting anything new, we'll update this page before it ships and change the date at the top.
