Support

Quick answers to the common questions. Still stuck? Email help.saath@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Getting started

How do I set up our home?

Open Saath, tap Create our home, pick your name and emoji, and name your home. Your iPhone needs to be signed in to iCloud (Settings › your name). That's it: no account to make.

How do I invite my partner or family?

On Home, tap Invite your person (or Settings › Invite someone) and send the link by Messages or WhatsApp. They install Saath, tap the link, pick a name and emoji, and you share one home. Each person needs their own Apple Account signed in to iCloud.

The invite link isn't opening Saath

Make sure Saath is installed on their iPhone first, and that they're signed in to iCloud. Then tap the link again. If it still doesn't open, send a fresh invite from Settings › Invite someone.

Groceries and lists

How do I add several groceries at once?

Separate them with commas: "milk, bread, 2 kg rice". Saath sorts each one into its aisle. Tap an aisle in an item's details to change it, and Saath remembers your choice.

Why didn't I get a notification?

Saath doesn't notify you about your own changes, and quick changes arrive together as one note. Check Settings › Notifications in Saath, and that notifications are allowed for Saath in iOS Settings.

Calendar

How do I connect Google Calendar?

Saath shows the calendars on your iPhone. Add your Google account in iOS Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Add Account › Google, with Calendars switched on. Then open Saath's Calendar tab, allow access, and tick the calendars you want to share.

Apple TV wall screen

Do I need an Apple TV?

No. Saath works fully on your iPhones. The wall screen is an optional extra if you have an Apple TV.

How do I set up the wall screen?

Install Saath on your Apple TV from the App Store and sign the TV in to iCloud with the Apple Account of someone in your home (Settings › Users and Accounts). Open Saath and it finds your home on its own. Choose the panels, orientation, colours and sizes on your iPhone in Settings › Wall screen layout.

My TV hangs in portrait and the screen is sideways

On your iPhone, open Settings › Wall screen layout › How the TV hangs, choose Portrait, then switch between Clockwise and Anticlockwise until it reads the right way up.

Purchases

How much does Saath cost?

Saath is free for 7 days with everything included. After that, a one-time purchase of $9.99 (or the local equivalent) unlocks it for good. There's no subscription.

Do I need to buy it twice for iPhone and Apple TV, or for my partner?

No. One purchase covers iPhone and Apple TV for your Apple Account, and is shared with your family through Family Sharing.

I bought Saath but it's asking me to unlock again

Open Saath › Settings › Saath and tap Restore purchase, using the Apple Account that made the purchase. For Family Sharing, make sure purchase sharing is on in iOS Settings › your name › Family Sharing.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Where is our data stored?

In your own iCloud, shared only with the people you invite. We can't see it. See the privacy policy.

How do I leave or delete our home?

In Saath, open Settings and scroll to the bottom. The person who created the home can delete it for everyone; anyone else can leave it.
