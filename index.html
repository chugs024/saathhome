Everything you share lives in one calm app on both your phones. Change something on one, it's on the other in seconds.

Groceries, sorted by aisle

Type "milk, bread, 2 kg rice" and Saath files each one where you'll find it in the store.

  • Understands what you call things, in English, Hindi or Gujarati.
  • The usuals: your regulars, one tap to add.
  • Walk into your store and Saath shows what's on the list.

Chores that take turns

Saath remembers whose turn it is. Tap Done and it passes to the next person, on the same day each week even if it's done late.

  • A friendly reminder on the morning it's yours.
  • Undo if you tapped too soon.

To-dos that matter

Give a to-do a date, an importance and a person when you add it. Filter by what's high, medium or low.

Never miss a bill

Rent, electricity, internet and the rest, with reminders the day before. See what's due in the next 30 days, and who paid last.

Your shared week

Saath shows the calendars on your iPhone, Google ones included, and adds plans to the one you share. Mark who's going and they show up beside each plan.

Leave a little note

"Made chipotle sauce for your lunch. Have a great day!" A post-it for the people at home, on their phone and on the kitchen TV.
