Questions

Do we need an Apple TV?

No. Saath works fully on your iPhones. If you have an Apple TV, the wall screen app is included at no extra cost.

Does each of us have to buy it?

One purchase covers your Apple Account on iPhone and Apple TV, and everyone in your Family Sharing group.

Where is our data kept?

In your own iCloud, shared only with the people you invite. Saath has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. Read the privacy policy.

Does it work with Google Calendar?

Yes. Saath shows the calendars on your iPhone, so a Google calendar added in Settings shows up too.

Which languages does the grocery list understand?

English, Hindi and Gujarati names for everyday groceries, so dhana, bhinda and doodh land in the right aisle.

What happens after the free week?

Buy Saath once for $9.99 to keep using it. Nothing renews and nothing is charged automatically.
